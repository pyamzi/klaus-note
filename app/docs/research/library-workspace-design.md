## Layout

The initial desktop workspace is a three-column grid with relative proportions (24/46/30), below a shared top bar containing navigation and Files/Reader/Card controls. Files, Reader, and Card can be hidden individually, with at least one pane retained. The two dividers provide pointer and keyboard resizing while all three desktop panes are visible. These proportions are a workspace default, not an app-wide grid token. [Workspace state, grid, and resize handlers](../../src/routes/library/+page.svelte), [direction contract](library-workspace.md), [desktop light](../../.impeccable/review/library/desktop.png).

At widths up to (899px), the route switches to a single full-width pane selected by Files, Reader, or Card. Pane visibility changes preserve the iframe instance in the DOM. The shared navigation has its own existing large-breakpoint menu behavior, independent of the workspace breakpoint; below that breakpoint the pane controls wrap into a second row within the same header. [Workspace responsive logic](../../src/routes/library/+page.svelte), [shared navigation](../../src/routes/StudyChrome.svelte), [mobile Reader](../../.impeccable/review/library/mobile.png), [mobile Card](../../.impeccable/review/library/mobile-card.png).

The repeated spacing rhythm comes from the incumbent utilities: close tool gaps, modest (8px to 16px) horizontal padding, compact rows, and borders between pane headers and controls. File rows indent by folder depth and truncate long names while retaining their title attribute. The reader fits the source page to its measured pane width at normal zoom and uses its own scrollable content region. [Workspace spacing and tree](../../src/routes/library/+page.svelte), [reader viewport](../../src/lib/library/PdfReader.svelte), [page viewport calculation](../../src/lib/library/PdfPage.svelte).

**The Persistent Panes Rule.** Files, Reader, and Card are views of one continuing workspace. Switching the visible pane does not create a replacement editor. [PDF content styling](../../src/lib/library/PdfPage.svelte), [mobile Reader](../../.impeccable/review/library/mobile.png).
